While working on a multi-tentant SAAS project, 
I lead the integration of semantic searches to their existing property leads search engine. 

Anywhere is the parent company of brands like: 
1) Coldwell Banker
2) Century 21
3) Sotheby's
4) Better Homes & Gardens. 

These were different tenants in our application, where real estate agents would sign up and do 
semantic searches like: 
1) Show me homes in quiet neighborhood in a particular zip code. 
2) Homes with great school ratings within particular price range. 

We had the leads in postgress SQL database with information about zip codes, price ranges, metadata. 
We enabled pg-vectors to create vector embeddings for the metadata. 

I took a hybrid approach combining both BM-25 and semantic searches to find the results and feed it to
the LLM for final user response. 

I was responsible for setting up websockets to stream the semantic search results to the user. 

I set guardrails and made sure that LLM is not halucinating. 

I used Langchain to create open-ai embeddings and for searching the leads.
